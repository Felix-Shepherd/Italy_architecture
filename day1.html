Goetheanum

Goetheanum (1928) was built by Rudolf Steiner after the first version of Goetheanum was burnt down on New Year's Eve 1922.

Steiner's philosophy of anthroposophy made him believe the concrete used to create a building should be moulded by like it was by hand. It signaled the future of sustainable and survivable buildings to be made of concrete, and it stands as a point proven in today's architecture.


The building itself is a symmetrical piece with a huge variation of surfaces that are shaped as curvature, lines, and other unconventional organic shapes all throughout the structure. In itself, the building seems abstract, but on the inside it was obviously made to be efficient from the main 'hub' to traverse through to every corner of the building.
