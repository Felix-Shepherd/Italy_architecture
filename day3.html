Galleria dell'Accademia di Firenze

analasis of the columns in Accademia

The columns of the Gallery are about one width away from the walls in a perfect square. We can see this because the ceiling has a cross from a diagonal of every column that meets in the middle.

Individually they are round and have a smaller diameter the longer up we look to make an illusion of more height than what actually stands. They have small rings near the ends and they become a larger square plate on the top. They are about 4 meters apart and about 5 meters tall.
